## Description (4,000 max)

> Quest! turns Victoria into a weekly game.
>
> Every quest is a real place and a real thing to do there. Get a flat white at Moss Street
> Market. Walk Dallas Road to Clover Point. Ride a harbour ferry. Try the soft-serve at Beacon
> Drive-In.
>
> How it works
>
> Pick a quest and go. When you’re inside its zone, take a photo of the place. That’s your proof.
> Quest! checks you’re there, and the XP and any badge land a few seconds later. No waiting for
> someone to approve it.
>
> Add a quick selfie if you like. It stays private.
>
> New quests drop on Monday morning. Some end on Sunday night, when the weekly rankings settle.
> Keep a streak going week to week and climb the board.
>
> What you get
>
> - Badges, medals and trophies, earned on the spot.
> - A sticker for each quest you finish: a real photo of the thing you did, cut out. Arrange them
>   on your own page.
> - This week, a short plan for what to do next, nearest first.
> - City, where everyone’s proof photos show up. Like, comment and follow people.
> - Rankings, this week’s board for the whole city.
> - A Sunday recap of your week, ready to share.
>
> Your first quest works anywhere, so you can try it before you head out.
>
> Got an idea for a quest? Send it in. If it goes live, it carries your name.
>
> Your call on privacy
>
> Location is only read while the app is open, to show what’s near you and check you’re in a
> quest’s zone. Other players see which quest you did, never your exact location. You can keep your photos off the public feed, report any post or comment,
> block anyone, and delete your account from Settings.
>
> Quest! is in its pilot in Victoria, BC.

(about 1,600)
