## The outside-Victoria check

Asked: can a brand-new account outside Victoria reach and complete the starter quest?
**In code, yes.** Full trace in APP-REVIEW.md §5:

- Onboarding has no city pick (`app/onboarding.tsx`). It stores `Victoria, BC` without asking.
- Nothing gates the account or app on real location. The Victoria boundary only applies to
  `city` quests.
- This week shows the `is_starter` quest to any account with no completions, whatever its
  position (`hooks/useThisWeek.ts`, `app/(tabs)/index.tsx`).
- The quest page and the camera screen don't gate a `none` quest. With no GPS fix or no
  permission, the attempt uses the quest's own coordinates (`app/submit/[questId].tsx`).
- The server's `check_completion_geofence` returns true for `none` before it reads coordinates
  (025). `normalize_completion_on_insert` approves it straight away (043, same limits as 038).
- Rate limits are 2 per 10 minutes and 10 per 24 hours. One quest is far under.

**No P0 in code.** P1-1 below turns into a P0 if production has no active `none` starter.

### P1: likely rejection, or blocks the loop on hosted data

**P1-1. The starter quest has to exist on production, and it has to have no zone.**
037 marks the starter by exact title (`Write a Journal Entry About Your City`, `geofence_type =
'none'`) and only if that row exists. `set_starter_quest` (037) accepts any active quest and
doesn't check `geofence_type`, so admin can move the starter onto a quest with a zone. Either way,
a reviewer outside Victoria would have nothing they can finish. This is a P0 if it's true on
production.
- Owner check (read-only, SQL editor):
  `select id, title, geofence_type, status, active_from, active_until from quests where is_starter;`
  Expect exactly one row, `geofence_type = none`, `status = active`, open window.
- Code follow-up (a DB card, not K2): make `set_starter_quest` refuse a quest whose
  `geofence_type` isn't `none`, and have the admin starter control say why.

**P1-2. Sign-up doesn't ask players to agree to the terms.**
Guideline 1.2 asks apps with user-generated content to have users agree to terms that make clear
there's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. `app/(auth)/sign-up.tsx` has no
terms line or checkbox, and onboarding doesn't mention terms. `app/legal/terms.tsx` has a
"Community & objectionable content" section but doesn't say "no tolerance", and doesn't commit to
acting on reports within 24 hours. This is a common rejection.
- Fix (UI card): a line under Create account, "By creating an account you agree to the Terms
  and the community rules", with both linked. Tighten the terms section. Files: `sign-up.tsx`,
  `terms.tsx`.

**P1-3. The privacy policy URL and contact may not be live, and the policy is out of date.**
`LEGAL_URLS.privacyPolicy` is `https://quest.app/privacy` and the contact is `hello@quest.app`
(`lib/constants.ts`, `app/legal/privacy.tsx`). The build-3 plan's DB3-7 says there's no
marketing domain yet. App Store Connect needs a privacy policy URL that opens, and Settings →
"Privacy policy on the web" would open a dead page. The in-app policy also says proof photos are
in a private bucket (they're public and show on City), says zones are "about 300 metres", says
email may be used for updates "if you opt in" (there's no opt-in), and leaves out selfies,
avatars, push tokens, Sentry, feedback, comments, follows and suggestions. List in
PRIVACY-LABELS.md §4.
- Owner: confirm the domain, or host the policy and a support page on a domain you control.
- Code: `privacy.tsx` belongs to N4. Update the text and `LEGAL_URLS` together, before or with N4.

**P1-4. Account deletion hasn't been checked on production, and it may fail.**
`delete_own_account` (032) deletes rows from `storage.objects` with SQL before deleting the auth
user. Hosted Supabase has been moving to block direct deletes from the storage tables, and to
require the Storage API instead. I couldn't confirm whether this project blocks it. If it does,
the RPC raises, Settings shows "Your account wasn't deleted", and Guideline 5.1.1(v) fails. Even
where the delete runs, deleting the row may leave the stored file behind. The runbook's
"Account deletion verified on a device" box is still open.
- Owner: run runbook Step 5 "Account deletion E2E" on a TestFlight build before submitting.
- If it fails: a DB card moves file removal to the Storage API (an edge function or client
  `remove()` before the RPC).

### P2: fix soon, or before Android

**P2-1. With an empty feed, the reviewer can't find Report or Block.**
Both live in the "..." menu on other players' posts. Your own post shows Share instead
(`components/FeedBubble.tsx`). APP-REVIEW.md §2 has you post one example from a second account.

**P2-2. Android mock location blocks the starter quest too.**
`canShoot` in `app/submit/[questId].tsx` requires `!isMocked` for every quest, including `none`
ones. A Play reviewer on an emulator, or anyone with a mock-location app, can't finish the
starter. iOS isn't affected in practice. Fix when Android ships (G5): skip the mock check for
`none` quests. The server doesn't use the coordinates for them anyway.

**P2-3. The build will carry generic purpose strings for permissions the app never asks for.**
Expo plugin defaults add `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` (`expo-camera`, `expo-image-picker`),
`NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription`, `NSLocationAlwaysUsageDescription` and
`NSMotionUsageDescription` (`expo-location`), each as "Allow Quest! to …". Unused strings aren't
shown, but they look careless to a reviewer and invite questions about background location.
Also, the camera string doesn't mention the selfie, and the location string doesn't mention
sorting quests by distance.
- Fix (`app.json`, next native build): `microphonePermission: false` on both camera plugins;
  `locationAlwaysAndWhenInUsePermission: false`, `locationAlwaysPermission: false` and
  `motionUsagePermission: false` on `expo-location`. Suggested copy: camera "Quest! uses your
  camera for proof photos of the place and an optional selfie."; location "Quest! uses your
  location to show quests near you and check you're in a quest's zone." Confirm in the built
  `Info.plist`.

**P2-4. Google Play needs a web page for account-deletion requests.**
Play's policy asks apps with accounts for a web link where people can ask for deletion without
the app. There isn't one. Fix with G5: a page on a domain you control.

**P2-5. Analytics has no opt-out, and PostHog may store coarse location from IP.**
`optOutAnalytics` exists in `lib/analytics.ts` but nothing calls it (Spec 04 §4 deferred the
toggle). The labels therefore mark analytics as required. PostHog derives city-level location
from IP unless "Discard client IP data" is on. Owner: turn it on in runbook Step 2. Later: the
Settings toggle.

**P2-6. A reviewer's photo goes on the public pilot feed by default.**
`feed_public` defaults to true (018). APP-REVIEW.md §2 step 5 has you switch it off on the demo
account.

**P2-7. The dev-only demo account has its password in the repo, and dev builds point at
production.** `handleDemoSignIn` in `app/(auth)/sign-in.tsx` is gated by `__DEV__`, so release
builds never show it. But `eas.json` points development builds at the production project, so
the account may exist there. Anyone who reads the repo can sign in as it and post to the public
feed. Owner: check whether it exists in production Auth and delete it if so. Code: read the dev
credentials from an untracked env file, or point dev builds at a non-production project.
